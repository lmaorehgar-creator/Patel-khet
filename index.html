Sentinel-2 satellite, 10 m resolution, a new picture roughly every 5 days. Cloudy passes are skipped. NDVI: under 0.2 bare soil, 0.2–0.4 young or sparse crop, 0.4–0.6 growing, above 0.6 dense and healthy. A steady drop in a growing crop is the thing to watch.

Soil moisture is a weather-model estimate for this spot, not a sensor reading. Rough guide: below 15% dry, 15–30% fine, above 40% waterlogged. NDMI higher means the crop holds more water. Standing water is detected from the satellite.
